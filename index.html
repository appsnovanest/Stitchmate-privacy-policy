Privacy policy

Your projects stay on your phone.

StitchMate is a row counter and project keeper for knitters and crocheters. It has no account and no server of its own. This page explains, in plain words, what the app stores, what the free version's ads and Google Play purchases involve, and the choices you have.

At a glance

No account, no server

You never sign up. Your projects, counts, notes, photos and yarn stash are saved only on your phone.

We never see your data

We (the developer) don't collect, receive or sell anything you create in the app.

Ads in the free version

Google AdMob shows ads and may use your device's advertising ID. Where the law requires it, you're asked first. Premium has no ads.

Who we are

StitchMate is made by a small independent developer ("we", "us"). We are responsible for the app. For anything in this policy, email appsnovanest@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

Everything you create in StitchMate is saved in the app's private storage on your phone. It is not sent to us or to anyone else. This includes:

  • projects, row and stitch counters, and counting history
  • notes, journal entries and reminders
  • your yarn stash and the calculator values you enter
  • photos you take or pick, and pattern files (PDFs or images) you add
  • statistics such as time spent and rows counted
  • your settings, such as theme, sounds, vibration and crochet terms

Other apps can't read this storage. We can't either.

Backups

Android backup
If Android backup is turned on, Android may copy StitchMate's database and settings to your own Google account. Photos and pattern files are left out of this copy. When you move to a new phone with Android's transfer tool, photos are included too. Google runs this service, not us, and you control it in your phone's settings.
Back up to a file
With Settings → Back up to a file, StitchMate writes a .zip file to a place you choose, for example Downloads or Google Drive. The file holds your projects and photos. Keep it somewhere safe. Restoring only reads a file you pick.

Permissions

StitchMate only asks for what a feature needs:

Notifications
Row reminders and the notification you use to turn off the floating counter.
Display over other apps
Only for the optional Floating Counter Bubble, so you can count while a pattern or video is open in another app. You turn it on yourself in system settings.
Foreground service
Keeps the floating counter running while another app is in front.
Vibration
A gentle buzz when you count and when a reminder fires.
Internet and advertising ID
Added by Google's ads and Play Billing libraries to load ads and check purchases. StitchMate doesn't use the internet for anything else.

StitchMate doesn't request camera permission. Photos are taken by your phone's own camera app, and pictures are chosen with Android's photo picker, which only shares the pictures you select. The app does not access your contacts, location, microphone or phone calls.

Advertising

The free version shows banner, full-screen and optional "watch a video" ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID and app-set ID
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • device and app information, such as model, OS version and language
  • how you interact with ads, such as views and taps

Google handles this data, not us. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your ad choices

  • UK, EEA and Switzerland: before any ad loads, Google's consent form asks whether you agree to personalised ads. You can change your answer at any time in Settings → Privacy and ad choices.
  • Everywhere: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings, under Settings → Privacy → Ads. The exact path varies by device.
  • No ads at all: StitchMate Premium removes every ad.

Ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences (rated PG).

Purchases

Premium subscriptions and the one-time lifetime purchase are processed by Google Play. We never see your name, card or payment details. The app only receives a confirmation from Google Play that a purchase is active and remembers it on your phone, so Premium works offline. Google's handling of payments is covered by the Google Privacy Policy. You can manage or cancel subscriptions in the Play Store app under Payments & subscriptions.

Sharing

When you share a progress card or export something, StitchMate creates the image or file on your phone and hands it to the app you pick in Android's share menu. Nothing is shared unless you choose to share it.

Emailing us

If you use Settings → Contact support or write to us directly, we receive your email address and whatever you write. To help us fix problems, the app adds its version, your Android version and your phone model to the draft. You can see this in the email and delete it before sending. We only use your email to reply to you. We don't add you to mailing lists, and we delete support emails on request.

Children

StitchMate is a general-audience crafting app and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, contact us and we'll delete it.

Keeping and deleting data

Your data stays on your phone until you delete it. You can delete individual projects in the app, clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall StitchMate. Uninstalling removes everything the app stored. Backup files you saved yourself stay where you saved them until you delete them. Android backups in your Google account are managed in your Google account settings.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as the GDPR and UK GDPR, and California's CCPA/CPRA, give you rights to access, correct, delete or move your personal data, to object to its use, and to withdraw consent. We don't hold your app data, so you can exercise most of these rights directly on your phone. For ad data, use the ad choices above or contact Google. For anything we might hold, such as your support emails, write to us. We'll reply within 30 days. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

We do not sell your personal information.

Security

Your data is kept in StitchMate's private app storage and protected by Android's app sandbox and your phone's lock screen. Connections made by Google's ads and billing libraries are encrypted. No method of storage is perfect, so please keep your phone secured and your backup files in a safe place.

Changes to this policy

If the app's handling of data changes, we'll update this page and change the effective date at the top. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.
